# ADR-039: DOE skylights by atlas family, from each family's source geometry

- **Status:** Accepted; decision 4 amended by D-252 (2026-11-01)
- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Decision log:** [D-249](decision-log.md#d-249--doe-skylights-by-atlas-family-from-each-familys-source-geometry-the-owners-g6-adr-039), [D-250](decision-log.md#d-250--the-prototype-check-of-code-rule-daylighting-run-again-on-the-retail-and-the-warehouse-with-their-skylights), [D-252](decision-log.md#d-252--doe-skylights-spread-as-the-source-does-amends-adr-039-decision-4)
- **Design note:** [stage BG4](../plans/2026-11-01-bg4-maps-geometry.md) §3 and §4; [stage BG5](../plans/2026-11-01-bg5-skylight-layouts.md) for decision 4
- **Amends:** [ADR-036](ADR-036-atlas-program-maps.md) decision 7 (an atlas map's presets keep the DOE reference building's glazing) for skylights

## Context

BEMGen's DOE layout records take their geometry and glazing from one source: the DOE commercial reference buildings v1.4, new construction (2004), Chicago (D-160). Their skylights are those of that file: the schools' gyms only. A DOE *Programs* component's atlas map kept that glazing for every family (ADR-036 decision 7), so the 90.1 and IECC retail and warehouse had no skylights, while their source models have them; BG3's prototype check of code-rule daylighting found the controlled floor fractions 0.066 and 0.052 against PNNL's 0.698 and 1.000 (D-235). The owner's answer G6 (2026-11-01, "follow the source evidence"): add skylights only where the source geometry carries them, per family where they differ.

## Decision

1. **Each family's skylights are its source geometry's.** The families' source models: DOE Ref pre-1980 and 1980–2004 the DOE Ref v1.4 pre-1980 and post-1980 IDFs (16 climates); 90.1-2007, 2013, 2019 and IECC 2006 to 2021 the PNNL prototype IDFs (19 climates); the supermarket, which PNNL does not model, the geometry OSM openstudio-standards names for the 90.1 template. `scripts/doe-geometry/family_skylights.py` reads every file, sums the skylight area of each program key's source zones, groups the climates with the same skylights, and writes `src/Lod.Generators/Doe/Skylights/family-skylights.json` (embedded, `--check` reproduces it byte for byte). The ratio is over the key's source roof area of the answer key, the basis of the record's own ratio.
2. **What they say.** No DOE Ref pre-1980 or 1980–2004 model has a skylight, not even the schools'. 90.1-2007 and IECC 2006 and 2009: the retail core 6.689 m², the warehouse bulk storage 14.8645 m², the schools' gyms as the records. 90.1-2013, 2019, IECC 2015 to 2021: the retail core 23.7832 m², the warehouse bulk 47.5664 and fine storage 20.8103 m², except in 6A, 6B, 7A, and 8A, where the models keep the 90.1-2004/2007 skylights (openstudio-standards' `model_custom_geometry_tweaks` of the retail and the warehouse does the same); IECC 2012: the retail core 23.7832 m² and the warehouse bulk 71.3495 and fine 25.2696 m² in every climate. The supermarket's sales 35.6748 m² in 90.1-2013 and 2019. No other type has a skylight in any family. The openstudio-standards template geometries agree with the PNNL models' groups outside 6A to 8A.
3. **Where they are used.** `DoeProgramPresets.KeyRatios(type, key, template, climateZone)` keeps the record's window ratios and takes the family's skylight ratio in the climate zone. A climate zone outside every group, or none, takes the template's own geometry (the group of the most climates) with `ClimateZoneAssumed`. The DOE *Programs* components use it for an atlas map (the map's family and the *Climate Zone* input, with a warning when the zone is assumed); the test-suite generator's `record` glazing uses it for a DOE slot whose programs are of one family (their JSON's atlas ID). The illustrative presets keep the record's glazing (the New2004 file's).
4. **The skylights' layout is the source's** (amended by D-252; it was BEMGen's, one centred skylight per ceiling, S5). `family_skylights.py` also records, per key and skylight ratio, the source's skylights as rectangles in the frame of the key's source roof (`layouts` in `family-skylights.json`, format version 2, with where each was found; the layout record's New2004 gyms equal the PNNL ones). A DOE generator's zone whose preset has a source's ratio gets that layout: the frame mapped onto the zone's roof's bounding box, each skylight's centre scaled with the roof, every skylight scaled alike to the key's skylight area (BG4's ratio × the roof); where one would leave the roof or meet another at the plan's dimensions, one centred skylight of that area with the warning `SkylightLayoutNotFitted`. Any other ratio keeps one centred skylight (S5). A roof that a later step leaves unchanged keeps the layout; a merged or split one takes one centred skylight of the area under it (ADR-012).

## Consequences

- An atlas map or a record-glazing suite of the retail, the warehouse, or the supermarket in a family whose source has skylights gets toplit roofs; the schools' gyms lose theirs in the DOE Ref families. The window area is unchanged everywhere.
- With code-rule daylighting on, the 90.1-2019 5A retail and warehouse match PNNL's controlled floor fraction (0.698 and 1.000). With one centred skylight they controlled less lighting (0.090 against 0.387; 0.208 against 0.492, D-250); with the source's layout (D-252) 0.402 and 0.618: the toplit areas are the source's, and what is left is the code rule's geometric shares (0.515 retail core, 0.528 bulk, 0.710 fine storage) against PNNL's fixed 0.25 + 0.25, and the warehouse office (G4).
- The LOD study's published results, made with the record's glazing at BEMGen `8d2e3bd` on atlas 2.0.0 inputs, stay as they are (D-244).

## Alternatives

- **One layout record per family.** Rejected: the families share the DOE Ref plan and zones; only the skylights differ, and the atlas maps already carry the family.
- **openstudio-standards' geometry for every 90.1 family.** Equivalent outside 6A to 8A; the PNNL models are the atlas's prototype source for the IECC families and carry the climate variants as written files, so they are read for 90.1 too, and openstudio-standards only where PNNL has no model.
- **The source's skylight count and spacing.** Not taken in BG4: it changes the generators' skylight rule (S5), which G6 did not ask for. Taken in BG5 (D-252, decision 4), on the owner's principle "follow the source evidence".
- **Fitting a regular grid of the source's count and size to any roof** (D-252). Rejected: the sources put the retail's, warehouse's, and supermarket's skylights in one part of the roof, not over the whole of it; a grid over the roof would light more of it than the source does.
